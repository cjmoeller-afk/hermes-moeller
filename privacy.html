Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Hermes Moeller is a private household assistant. Its only users are members of one household, who authorize it with their own Google accounts.

What data it accesses

With the user's permission, it reads Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Drive files, and can, only with the user's explicit approval each time, send email, change calendar events or change Drive files.

How data is used

Data is used only to produce schedules, reminders, summaries and answers for the same household. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models offered to others.

Where data is stored

Data and access tokens are stored on the household's own computer. Requests may be processed by the household's chosen AI model providers solely to generate responses for the household.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/cjmoeller-afk/hermes-moeller.
